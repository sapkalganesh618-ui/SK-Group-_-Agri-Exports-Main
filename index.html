Buyer Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is your minimum order quantity?
We primarily supply by full container load (1x 20 ft or 40 ft FCL), though for specific high-value commodities or trial sample shipments, partial load (LCL) terms can be arranged upon discussion.
Which shipping terms do you quote?
We quote standard Incoterms including FOB (Free on Board from major Indian ports like Mundra, JNPT, or Chennai) and CIF (Cost, Insurance, and Freight) to your designated arrival seaport.
Can I get a sample before ordering?
Yes, representative pre-shipment samples can be dispatched via international courier (DHL/FedEx) for your lab analysis, moisture check, and grading approval prior to contract signing.
Which export documents do you provide?
Every shipment is accompanied by a Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Clean On-Board Bill of Lading, Certificate of Origin, Phytosanitary Certificate, and third-party inspection (SGS/Bureau Veritas) certificates upon request.
What payment terms do you accept?
Standard B2B terms include Irrevocable Confirmed Letter of Credit (LC at sight) or advance telegraphic transfer (T/T deposit with balance against copy of Bill of Lading).
